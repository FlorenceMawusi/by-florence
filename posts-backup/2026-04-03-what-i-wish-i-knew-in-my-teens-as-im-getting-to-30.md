# What I Wish I Knew in My Teens as I’m getting to 30

[![](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!GwYf!,w_1456,c_limit,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2F62d4895b-0faf-43ea-b1c1-8047cb88eed3_4032x3024.jpeg)](<https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!GwYf!,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2F62d4895b-0faf-43ea-b1c1-8047cb88eed3_4032x3024.jpeg>)

**1\. Connect More With People.**

I think I should have connected more with people in the world rather than being so focused on books and grades.

Grades got me far, like really far, but **I think I could have done less with the grades and maybe traded that for connection, getting to know peoples worlds better.**

**2\. Believe in Myself and My Gifts**

I should believe in myself more and know that I’m such an intelligent being with so many gifts and talents, and I should really believe in them and work at growing those talents.

Because yeah, they were worth growing or they were worth seeing through.

**I should have started writing a long time ago, and I should have pursued it relentlessly without fear or perfection in mind.**

**3\. Listen to Myself**

> **I should tune out what everyone else was saying and listen to myself and what I want, and use that as my blueprint in the world.**

Like always doing what I want. That way I would not people please as much and would have been safer and happier.

**4\. It’s Okay to Make Mistakes**

I wish I knew that it was okay to make mistakes and not be perfect, and that’s what being human meant. 

**Above all this is what I’d want my future self, even my current self to remember.**

In summary I’d tell her to keep being herself, and to keep figuring life even when it felt confusing. She didn’t have the answer to all questions, but **what mattered is she kept moving and believing in herself.**
