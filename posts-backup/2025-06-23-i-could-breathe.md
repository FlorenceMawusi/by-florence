# I Could Breathe

[![](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!6eKd!,w_1456,c_limit,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2F6f5120dd-df26-40b9-95aa-01196e92d555_1024x1024.jpeg)](<https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!6eKd!,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2F6f5120dd-df26-40b9-95aa-01196e92d555_1024x1024.jpeg>)

My chest opened up

and my breath got heavy

my airways

they gave room

for my heavy breaths

And I knew I was lighter

and happier

and grateful

and peaceful

They saw who I was

behind the calm

the whirlwind underneath the softness

the dread and the fear

and they named it

and stayed with it

and mirrored it

and breathed with it

We met

in the temple of expression

our creativity

soft fabrics, bold colors

hair coiled with intention

exotic jewelry catching the light

They read me

and I let them

and I was opened up

and my fears rose to the surface

and they stood with me

in all the mess

sitting in front of us

And we said what we felt

that we wanted life more soft

and didn’t want to keep pushing

We wanted to be pulled into life

and to sway our hips to life’s music

ever so melodious

like a live band at Sunday brunch

So I left

with a heart as light as a feather

and healing

only my body could understand

My mind hadn’t caught up

but my windpipe knew

and cleared the airways

so I could breathe

Because what my body perceived,

my thoughts were too slow to name:

This is healing.
