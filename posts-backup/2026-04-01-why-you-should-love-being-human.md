# Why You Should Love Being Human
_Embracing our divinity_

[![woman wearing black sunglasses and black camisole](https://images.unsplash.com/photo-1568782517100-09bf22d88c2d?crop=entropy&cs=tinysrgb&fit=max&fm=jpg&ixid=M3wzMDAzMzh8MHwxfHNlYXJjaHw3fHxkaXZpbmUlMjBodW1hbiUyMGJsYWNrfGVufDB8fHx8MTc3NTA3OTEzNnww&ixlib=rb-4.1.0&q=80&w=1080)](<https://images.unsplash.com/photo-1568782517100-09bf22d88c2d?crop=entropy&cs=tinysrgb&fit=max&fm=jpg&ixid=M3wzMDAzMzh8MHwxfHNlYXJjaHw3fHxkaXZpbmUlMjBodW1hbiUyMGJsYWNrfGVufDB8fHx8MTc3NTA3OTEzNnww&ixlib=rb-4.1.0&q=80&w=1080>)Photo by [Andrey Zvyagintsev](<https://unsplash.com/@zvandrei>) on [Unsplash](<https://unsplash.com>)

I was not allowed  
to be human

not allowed  
to be imperfect

not allowed  
to have fun

  
to be in my body  
to listen  
to my own voice

i was not allowed  
to want  
just because i wanted

* * *

every desire  
needed a 10-slide presentation  
a justification  
a reason good enough  
to be approved

and some things  
i couldn’t imagine wanting them

they were unspeakable  
taboo

so i learned  
to shrink my desires  
before they could even form

i knew, somewhere,  
something wasn’t right

and yet  
i thought everything was

because it was all i knew

* * *

but tell me

is that fair?

that others chase desire  
while you police yours

that they receive  
while you are too timid  
to even imagine asking

too conditioned  
from seeing  
you are worthy  
simply because you’re a being

* * *

what a beautiful thing it is  
to be human

to have so much power  
and to know it

to transform  
from observer  
to creator

to return  
to the self  
you were before permission  
was required

to remember  
your God-given capabilities  

is this not the good news?

the gospel?

that we are free indeed

that we were never bound  
only taught to believe we were

that the shackles  
were imagined  
repeated  
inherited

and now  
removed

* * *

open your eyes

no, really  
see

you are flawed  
yet powerful

you are biased  
and all the more beautiful

you have a heart  
 _of gold_

an intuition  
that drives you

what a thing, i proclaim  
what a thing

**to be human**
