# The Empty Map: Why You Don’t Know What You Want (And How to Start Choosing)
_Why I didn't know what I wanted for 20 years, and the 'imperfect' process of finally drawing my own map. _

[![](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!9IrT!,w_1456,c_limit,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2F066e6d96-6afa-4489-bada-d36d2025f2fc_1024x1024.png)](<https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!9IrT!,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2F066e6d96-6afa-4489-bada-d36d2025f2fc_1024x1024.png>)Made with Gemini

Have you ever been asked “Where do you want to eat?” and felt a genuine, hollow blankness in your mind?

For years, I thought I was just “easy-going.” I told myself I didn’t care about the restaurant, the movie, or the weekend plans. But after moving out of the **Vault** , I realized the truth: **I didn’t have a Preference Map.**

When you spend your life as a “Giant-Sizer,” having a preference is dangerous. If you want something different than the Giant (the boss, the parent, the partner), you risk conflict. To stay safe, your brain simply **deletes your desires** before you can even feel them.

**Why Your Map is Blank**

If you are a recovering people-pleaser, your “internal GPS” was likely disabled early on for survival.

  * **The Shape-Shifter Bias:** You became “liquid” to fit whatever container the Giants provided.

  * **Safety Over Choice:** In the Vault, the only “correct” preference is whatever keeps the peace. Over time, the neural pathways for _wanting_ actually atrophy.

  * **The Identity Blur:** Without a map, you don’t know where you end and others begin.

**Building the Map: The “Imperfect Action” Method**

You cannot “think” your way into having a personality. You have to **somaticize** it. Building your map is a manual process of trial and error.

  1. **Start with the Binaries:** Don’t ask “What is my soul’s purpose?” Ask: _“Does this coffee taste ‘Glowy’ (expansive) or ‘Vaulty’ (contracted)?”_

  2. **The 51% Rule:** If you feel even 1% more leaning toward Option A than Option B, **broadcast it.** Even if you aren’t sure. The act of choosing is more important than the choice itself.

  3. **Expect the “Shock”:** The first time you say “I’d actually prefer the other seat,” you might feel a “knee-jerk” reaction of fear. Stay in your **minty** limbs. Feel your **gravity**. The sky will not fall because you have an opinion.

  4. **Value the “Imperfect”:** Your map doesn’t have to be cool, trendy, or “right.” It just has to be yours. If you like “bad” music or “weird” hobbies, put them on the map. They are the landmarks of your soul.

**From Receiver to Creator**

Building a Preference Map is exhausting. It takes “high-voltage” energy to negotiate your space after years of being a “Gray Rock.” You might feel unmotivated for chores because your brain is busy **re-wiring your entire sense of self.**

But here is the reward: When you have a map, you have **Traction.** Life stops “slipping off” you. You stop being a ghost and start being a person who actually _lives_ in the world.

* * *

**💡 Glossary Update:**

  * **Preference Map:** The internal database of your authentic likes, dislikes, and “hell yes” moments.

  * **The Binary Test:** Using the “Glow” vs. “Vault” body sensations to make decisions when your mind feels blank.

  * **Unmapped State:** The “hollow” feeling of having no preferences, usually caused by long-term survival masking.

**Start Your Own Map!**

Building a preference map is an **imperfect action**.

  * **What is one tiny “Unmapped” area of your life?** (Maybe it’s what you actually want for dinner, or the music you listen to when no one is watching).

  * **What was your first “Binary Test” result today?** Did you choose the “Other Seat” or the “Glowy Coffee”?

  * **How does it feel to say “Mine”** about a choice, even if it “isn’t cool”?

**Meet me in the comments.** Let’s compare our maps and see what happens when we stop being receivers and start being creators.
