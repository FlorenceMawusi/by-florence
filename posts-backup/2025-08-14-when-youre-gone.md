# When you’re gone 

I know it’s coming

This time I know

What it’s going to be like

When you’re gone. 

At first I wouldn’t 

I’d think I’d be fine

On my own

When you’re gone 

But now I know

And can tell 

That it’ll be hollow 

And cloudy

And a little more silent 

When you’re gone

It hits me now

The spark you brought

Into my world 

We jumped high in the sky

And touched the moon

And landed back on our feet

And laughed till the stars wiggled 

We flew into the clouds

Scooped some cloud dust 

We threw it onto the ground

Creating a splatter of an ocean

We roamed the world 

And treaded whole lands

Both mountains and valleys 

But we still finished standing strong 

You’re my friend

My special friend 

And that won’t change 

Because we’ll be friends for life 

Someday and one day 

Our thirst will be quenched

In a way that will make us say

That we were worth it

And so was the wait. 

Notes: 

My friend is going away and I’m realizing how sad this is. Also reminiscing the times we shared and having hope for the future despite me missing her.
