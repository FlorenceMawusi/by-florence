# The Real Love Story

[![](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!o6Sh!,w_1456,c_limit,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2F065358e4-0fdc-4ba9-b512-3bfe9a2eb0a5_1024x1024.jpeg)](<https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!o6Sh!,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2F065358e4-0fdc-4ba9-b512-3bfe9a2eb0a5_1024x1024.jpeg>)

It’s not the push pull, 

not the highs and lows 

or the big, dramatic music swelling 

when one person almost leaves 

but then comes running back.

Rather

It’s the way every ordinary day 

becomes a soft place to land, 

and the quiet between you two feels like home.

It’s the morning walk 

past the same cracked sidewalk, 

where your conversations drift 

between grocery lists and dreams.

It’s the everyday closeness. 

The little post-it 

you find on the mirror

from your loved one 

when you’re heading out.

The flowers you bought together, 

still bright on the table, 

arranged exactly how you like them,

filling the room with life

It’s driving round the shopping mall together, talking about nothing and everything.

[![](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!0y4M!,w_1456,c_limit,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2F72c3096b-fdea-4d06-a831-4fd88a114f47_1024x1024.jpeg)](<https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!0y4M!,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2F72c3096b-fdea-4d06-a831-4fd88a114f47_1024x1024.jpeg>)

It’s shopping for groceries, 

then curling up on the couch to watch a movie you’ve both seen before.

It’s when cooking and cleaning,

no longer feel like chores,

because their presence,

make it a fun activity.

It’s knowing someone’s there, 

not just for the fireworks 

but for the quiet, steady days 

that make you feel safe enough 

to dream again.

It’s how those small moments 

heal you in ways 

Hollywood never talks about.
